About me

Building at the intersection of strategy and creativity

I'm a creative-strategic professional who bridges analytical thinking with hands-on execution. Currently completing my MSc in Global Sports Business Management and Digital Transformation at ESBS Madrid. My unique perspective comes from 10 years studying Japanese visual storytelling — understanding content pacing, audience engagement, and emotional arc design at a deep level.
